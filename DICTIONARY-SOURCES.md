# Dictionary sources

- The idiom glossary uses the [English Wiktionary idiomatic export via Kaikki](https://kaikki.org/dictionary/English/tags/93/idiomatic/index.html) under CC BY-SA 4.0 and Chinese glosses from ECDICT under MIT. Candidate phrases are ranked by occurrences in [Tatoeba English examples](https://tatoeba.org/en/downloads). Only aggregate counts inform ranking; example sentences are not distributed.

- [Simple English Wiktionary contributors](https://simple.wiktionary.org/) via [Kaikki/Wiktextract](https://kaikki.org/simplewiktionary/rawdata.html), October 2026 dump. WordTap selects English headwords, glosses and word-form references, filters some senses, and indexes the selected material in SQLite. The resulting Wiktionary material is provided under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Source entries and contributor histories are available from `https://simple.wiktionary.org/wiki/<headword>`.
- [ECDICT](https://github.com/skywind3000/ECDICT), by skywind3000 and contributors, MIT license. WordTap extracts translations, normalizes headwords and selects up to five Chinese gloss lines per entry. The 50,000-entry English core keeps the original 25,000 headwords selected by the modern-corpus `frq` rank and adds specialist terms and general vocabulary. Further general vocabulary uses `frq` and `bnc` ranks. Simple English Wiktionary supplies many general definitions, with original WordTap, ECDICT and Open English WordNet glosses filling gaps. Inflection links come from ECDICT's `exchange` field and Simple English Wiktionary's form descriptions. See ECDICT-LICENSE.txt.
- [English Wiktionary contributors](https://en.wiktionary.org/) via [Kaikki/Wiktextract's English topic exports](https://kaikki.org/dictionary/English/topics/Iz/philosophy/index.html), October 2026 dump. WordTap selects and indexes domain-tagged senses for philosophy, history, psychology and humanities. The extracted definitions are available under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Source entries and contributor histories are available from `https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/<headword>`.
- [Open English WordNet 2025+](https://en-word.net/downloads), by the Open English WordNet team, incorporating Princeton WordNet and curated entries from Open English Namenet. WordTap selects a subset of English glosses and adds part-of-speech labels. Open English WordNet contributions use [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Princeton WordNet material retains its original WordNet License. Both notices appear in WORDNET-LICENSE.md.
- [Loterre Philosophy](https://doi.org/10.13143/lotr.8205), [Prehistory and Protohistory](https://doi.org/10.13143/lotr.1723), [History of Science and Technology](https://doi.org/10.13143/lotr.6994), [History and Sciences of Religions](https://doi.org/10.13143/lotr.6867), [Literature](https://doi.org/10.13143/lotr.7763), and [Cognitive Psychology of Human Memory](https://doi.org/10.13143/lotr.5330), by the Institute for Scientific and Technical Information (Inist) – CNRS/UAR76, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). WordTap normalizes and selects English labels and a subset of the memory vocabulary's definitions, and adds available meanings from the sources above.
- [Wikidata contributors](https://www.wikidata.org/), [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). WordTap selects structured labels and brief descriptions where the English Wikipedia title or Wikidata label matches the Loterre term. Source entity IDs are retained in `specialist-wikidata.tsv`.
- WordTap contributes concise English and Chinese definitions for selected philosophy, history, humanities, and psychology terms in `specialist-overrides.tsv` and `specialist-loterre-overrides.tsv`.
